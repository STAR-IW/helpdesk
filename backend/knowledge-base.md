# Knowledge Base

Answers to common support questions. The AI auto-response feature uses these entries to draft replies. Each entry sits under a `##` heading, is grouped by ticket category, and lists keywords to help match it to a ticket.

---

# General Questions

## How do I contact support?
**Keywords:** contact, support, help, email, reach, talk to someone

Email our support address and a ticket is opened automatically. Reply to any message in the thread to add to that ticket instead of opening a new one. We usually answer within one business day.

## What are your support hours?
**Keywords:** hours, open, available, weekend, response time

Our agents work Monday to Friday, 9:00–17:00. Messages sent outside those hours are answered the next business day, in the order they arrived.

## How do I update my profile or email address?
**Keywords:** profile, update, change email, name, personal details

1. Sign in to your account.
2. Open **Account Settings** → **Profile**.
3. Change your name or email address and click **Save**.

If you change your email, we send a confirmation link to the new address, and the change only takes effect once you click it.

## How do I delete my account?
**Keywords:** delete account, close account, remove data, unsubscribe

Reply to this ticket and confirm that you want your account deleted. For security, the request has to come from the email address registered on the account. Deleting an account is permanent and removes your course progress and purchase history.

## How do I check the status of my ticket?
**Keywords:** ticket status, follow up, any update, still waiting

Replying to the original email thread keeps everything in one ticket. An agent will see your follow-up alongside the rest of the conversation. Please don't open a new ticket for the same issue, because a duplicate can slow down the response.

# Technical Questions

## How do I change my password?
**Keywords:** change password, update password, new password, pass

1. Sign in to your account.
2. Go to **Account Settings** → **Security**.
3. Enter your current password, then your new password twice.
4. Click **Update Password**.

Your new password must be at least 8 characters long. Changing it signs you out of every other device.

## I forgot my password. How do I reset it?
**Keywords:** forgot password, reset password, can't log in, lost password, pass

1. On the sign-in page, click **Forgot password?**
2. Enter the email address on your account.
3. Open the reset email and follow the link within 1 hour, before it expires.
4. Choose a new password.

If the email hasn't arrived after a few minutes, check your spam or junk folder. If it still isn't there, reply to this ticket and we'll help you directly.

## I can't log in to my account
**Keywords:** can't log in, login failed, locked out, sign in error, invalid credentials

- Check that you're using the email address you registered with.
- Passwords are case-sensitive, so make sure Caps Lock is off.
- Try resetting your password with **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page.
- Clear your browser's cookies and cache, or try a private/incognito window.

If none of these steps work, reply with the exact error message you see and the browser you're using.

## I'm not receiving emails from you
**Keywords:** no email, not receiving, missing email, verification email, spam

- Check your spam, junk, and promotions folders.
- Add our support address to your contacts or safe-senders list.
- Make sure the email address in your profile is correct.

If emails still don't arrive, reply to this ticket and we'll check our delivery logs.

## The site or video isn't loading
**Keywords:** not loading, video, buffering, blank page, error, slow, broken

1. Refresh the page.
2. Try another browser. We support the latest versions of Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge.
3. Turn off browser extensions, especially ad blockers.
4. Clear your browser's cache and cookies.
5. Check your internet connection. Video needs a stable connection of at least 5 Mbps.

If the problem continues, reply with your browser, device, and a screenshot of any error message.

## How do I download my certificate?
**Keywords:** certificate, completion, download certificate, diploma

Once you finish every required lesson in a course, your certificate appears under **My Courses** → *course name* → **Certificate**. Click **Download PDF** to save it. If your progress shows 100% but there's no certificate, reply to this ticket with the course name.

# Refund Requests

## How do I get a refund?
**Keywords:** refund, money back, return, cancel purchase, reimburse

You can request a refund within **14 days of purchase**, as long as you've completed less than 30% of the course. To request one:

1. Reply to this ticket with the email address you used to buy the course and the course name, plus your order number if you have it.
2. An agent checks that the purchase qualifies.
3. Approved refunds go back to your original payment method within 5–10 business days.

## Am I eligible for a refund?
**Keywords:** refund eligibility, refund policy, can I get my money back

You qualify for a refund if:
- you ask within 14 days of purchase, **and**
- you've completed less than 30% of the course.

Purchases outside these conditions usually don't qualify. If there were special circumstances, such as a technical problem that kept you from using the course, reply with the details and an agent will review your case.

## When will I receive my refund?
**Keywords:** refund status, refund not received, when refund, how long refund

After a refund is approved, it takes **5–10 business days** to show up on your original payment method, depending on your bank or card provider. If more than 10 business days have passed since approval, reply to this ticket and we'll look into it.

## I was charged twice
**Keywords:** double charge, charged twice, duplicate payment, overcharged

Sorry about that. Please reply with the date of the charges and the last 4 digits of the card you used. Never send your full card number. Once we confirm the duplicate charge, we refund it in full, and it reaches your account within 5–10 business days.

## How do I cancel my subscription?
**Keywords:** cancel subscription, stop billing, unsubscribe, recurring payment

1. Sign in and go to **Account Settings** → **Billing**.
2. Click **Cancel Subscription**.

You keep access until the end of the current billing period, and you won't be charged again. Subscription payments that have already been charged follow the same 14-day refund policy above.
